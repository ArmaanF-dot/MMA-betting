# Case FOR placing the bets (UFC Vegas 123, paper, 50 pound budget)

Shared evidence: 102 past cards, real prices, the same rule: +3.7% on stake, 95% range -10% to +17%, so zero is inside the range. Stakes here total 5.77 of 50. Expected value (EV) below is return per 1 staked at the best price. Card EV in pounds: +0.38 under the model's view, -0.14 under the market's.

## 1. Tom Nolan (Tom Nolan to beat Renato Moicano), 2.14 at BetRivers, stake 2.82
Blend 49.7% vs market 47.2%, edge +6.4%, no flags, 20 and 6 prior fights. EV is +6.4% on the blend, +27.6% on the model alone (59.7%), and only +1.0% on the market's number, so it loses nothing even if the market is right. The model pick agrees with the bet, and the price is the best of 5 books (median 2.00), so line shopping supplies much of the edge. Evidence: best-price Kelly bets on past cards returned +5.2% (433 bets, mean closing-line value +0.030). Objection: a 6-point edge is within noise. Answer: stake is small (2.82), a coin-flip price pays 1.14 for a win, and the downside is bounded.

## 2. Mickey Gall, 3.65 at BetRivers, stake 1.58
Blend 29.7% vs market 26.3%, edge +8.4%, no thin flag. EV +8.4% on the blend, +66% on the model (45.5%), -4.2% on the market. Objection: Gall has a 715-day layoff and Zach Reese's age is unknown, and the model's own "long layoff" driver is -4.9. Answer: the blend already shrinks the model from 45.5% to 29.7%, nearly to the market, so the layoff is largely priced. The bet needs only a 27.4% chance to break even at 3.65, and the market says 26.3%.

## 3. Theodor Berggren, 2.50 at Caesars, stake 0.91

## 4. Tainara Lisboa, 5.60 at Caesars, stake 0.46
Blend 19.4% vs market 15.5%, edge +8.4%. EV +8.4% blend, +133% model (41.7%), -13.5% market. Objection: Lucia Szabova is not in the data (treated as a debutant, age unknown), so the model's 58% for her is nearly blind, and a longshot at 5.60 loses about 4 times in 5. Answer: the stake is 0.46, the thin halving and the blend weight make this a lottery ticket sized as one. The blend moved the model's view from 41.7% down to 19.4%, so it is not trusting the model. Worst case: 0.46 lost.

## 5. Paper pick: Luana Santos, 1.37 at Caesars, 80+ tier (flat 10, paper only)
Model 84.0%, blend 74.2%, market 71.2%. EV +15.1% on the model, +1.7% on the blend, -2.4% on the market. The Kelly rule rightly passes (edge +1.7%, below 3%), so this is the separate test of C3_v1, registered before any card. Evidence: 80%+ model picks on 108 bets in 2017 to 2023-05 hit 85.2% (model 83.3%, market 77.3%), +5.5% mid price, +7.1% best price. The same direction held in the recent period (+5.5% to +10.2%), and favourites are underpriced (slope 1.156 on validation). Objection: each interval touches zero, the cutoff was scanned, and the rule's own plan demands at least 40 picks at 80+ before judging. Answer: agreed, which is why it is paper money; one pick at 1.37 costs nothing and adds a data point of the very kind the plan needs.

## Summary
Four small edges (+5% to +8%) are positive on the blend and the model, about neutral on the market, with only 5.77 at stake; the rule costs little if wrong and the forward log is the only way to settle it.
The past +3.7% has a range including zero, which we concede; paper bets are how to narrow it.
The Santos pick is a pre-registered test, not a bet on its own merits.
