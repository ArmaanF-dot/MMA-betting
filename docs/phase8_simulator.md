# Phase 8: simulator and judging model (public per-round data)

Generated by `python -m ufcmodel phase8`. Walk-forward: judging weights, finish scalars and direct models are refit per fold on bouts before it.

## What this is, and is not
- A round-level Monte Carlo simulator, not the spec's second-by-second positional semi-Markov model: positions need tagged sequences. Skills come from the as-of opponent-adjusted rates; fatigue is the fighter's late-round slope; damage accumulates within the fight; finish hazards use two scalars calibrated to the training finish rates.
- Judging: logistic on summed round stat differences predicts the decision winner; a temperature makes three independent judges reproduce decision-winner probabilities. No per-round judge scores exist, so 10-8 rounds and judge tendencies are not modelled.

Evaluation set: 5354 bouts from the first fold to the end of the development folds (5243 with a winner and a KO/submission/decision method).
